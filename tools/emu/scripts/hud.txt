# Straight into gameplay and hold there, for judging the HUD on its own.
#
# The shortest useful script: no menus, no transitions, just the frame the
# player spends all their time looking at.

wait 120
press A 8
wait 120
shot hud

# Swing the camera round, so the HUD is judged over a moving background rather
# than over whichever frame it happened to start on.
stick 80 0 60
wait 60
shot hud-turned

stop
