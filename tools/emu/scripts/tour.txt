# A tour of every screen the starter game reaches, one shot each.
#
# Durations count rendered frames, not wall clock: a heavy scene draws fewer of
# them per second, so a wait means "this many pictures", which is the unit that
# makes a capture reproducible.
#
# See docs/emulator-screenshots.md for the full grammar.

wait 120
shot title              # slot picker, legend, storage status

press DDOWN 6
wait 30
shot title-slot-two     # the cursor moved; check the highlight reads

press A 8
wait 90
shot play               # crate over the pedestal, HUD legend, hearts

press START 8
wait 60
shot pause              # options card, volume pips, check marks

press CLEFT 6
wait 30
shot pause-quieter      # one pip fewer, to check the on/dim states differ

press B 8
wait 60
press B 8
wait 90
shot saved              # the save confirmation line

stop
